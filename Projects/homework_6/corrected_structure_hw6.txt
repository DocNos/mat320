=============================

After getting the correct plot:

1. Find peaks using scipy.signal.find_peaks()
2. Identify 1st and 10th harmonic peaks
3. Calculate discrepancy:
   - Expected 10th harmonic: 10 * (frequency of 1st peak)
   - Actual 10th harmonic: measured frequency
   - Percentage error = 100 * (actual - expected) / expected

4. Convert to various units:
   - Hz: discrepancy_normalized * 44100
   - Ratio: actual_freq / expected_freq
   - Cents: (1200/ln(2)) * ln(ratio)
